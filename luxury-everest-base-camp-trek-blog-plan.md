# Luxury Everest Base Camp Trek — 6-Month Blog & SEO Content Plan

**Brand:** Everest Trip Planner
**Money page:** [Luxury Everest Base Camp Trek](https://everesttripplanner.com/luxury-everest-base-camp-trek) (`trip:luxury-everest-base-camp-trek`)
**Plan duration:** 6 months, October 2026 to March 2027
**Publishing frequency:** 6 posts a month (Tuesday every week, plus Friday every other week)
**Total planned articles:** 36 (3 already live)
**Primary objective:** Rank for the searches people make *before* they book a comfortable Everest trek, build topical authority around luxury Everest trekking, and send every reader to the luxury trip page and `/start-planning`.

**Tone rule (all posts):** Simple, positive and soft, as set out in `.claude/skills/write-blog/SKILL.md`. The reader can afford comfort but is probably a little nervous. Hard topics (altitude, cold, flight delays, age, the lodges above Dingboche being simpler) get the honest fact, then why it's normal, then what we do about it. Never end a section on alarm.

**Keyword ownership rule:** the trip page owns the bare phrase **"luxury everest base camp trek"**. Never make that exact phrase a post's primary keyword. Every post targets a modifier ("…cost", "…itinerary", "…for over 60s") or a neighbouring phrase ("everest base camp in comfort", "hotel everest view").

## 1. SEO Strategy

Six topic clusters, one per month:

1. **Luxury EBC Planning Fundamentals**
2. **Cost, Value & Booking**
3. **Comfort on the Trail**
4. **Helicopter, Flights & Kathmandu**
5. **Health, Preparation & Who It's For**
6. **Comparisons & Experience**

### Conversion pages

Every post links toward, in this order of importance:

- Luxury Everest Base Camp Trek — `trip:luxury-everest-base-camp-trek` (2-3 times, first within 200 words)
- Plan your trip — `/start-planning`
- Contact — `/contact-us`
- Secondary trips, only when the comparison is the point: `trip:everest-base-camp-trek-with-helicopter-return`, `trip:everest-base-camp-trek`, `trip:everest-heli-tour`, `trip:gokyo-valley-trek`

### Internal-link rule

Each post has **4-7 contextual internal links**:

- 2-3 to the luxury trip page (varied anchors, §4)
- 1-2 to live sibling posts in this plan
- 1 to a relevant `/about-everest/...` page
- 1 CTA to `/start-planning`

Link only to posts that are live. Trips always as `href="trip:{slug}"`, never as a path.

# 5. Content Checklist

The `write-blog` skill enforces most of this; listed here so nothing is missed:

- Keyword in the first sentence, "The short answer" section near the top
- 1,200-1,800 words, H2/H3 only (the page renders the H1)
- At least one table or list
- 4-7 internal links (§1), trip links as `trip:{slug}`
- FAQ with 3-4 questions
- Meta title ≤ 60 characters, meta description ≤ 155 characters, 1-2 sentence excerpt
- No price stated for our trip (`price` is null): market ranges only, clearly labelled
- Featured image picked in `/admin` → Blogs
- Shipped as an idempotent seeder: `php artisan db:seed --class={Name}BlogSeeder --force` on production
